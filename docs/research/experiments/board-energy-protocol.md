# Frozen gross-board energy replay control

NVML availability now exposes both the cumulative board-energy counter and reported power on this physical RTX 3060/595.95 driver. The [availability capture](nvml-availability.json) is an uncontrolled read, not an idle calibration or accuracy certificate. No clock, power limit, persistence setting, driver or unrelated process is changed.

Replay the unchanged frozen Fashion-MNIST CNN worker in its original CUDA environment, seed 1101, five epochs, three measurement pairs, both reference and checkpoint-plus-microbatch arms. Randomize arm order within each pair with seed 2101 before execution. These are resource-measurement replays of one existing seed, not six new independent learning seeds or a confirmatory energy study. Validate all initialization/split/order/final-state digests and every epoch's validation/update values against the original arm. Timing fields are expected to vary.

Read-only supervisor uses nvidia-ml-py 13.615.71 in a separate minimal environment. The installed binding's library-load/init/power/counter wrappers were inspected; its source hash is fixed by availability evidence. Sampling target is 50 ms; retain actual relative timestamps, query span, power mW, cumulative energy mJ, utilization, temperature and errors. No GPU UUID, other-process identities or arbitrary environment values are collected. Workers use private owned temporary/profile paths, explicit environment and the trusted original CLI with a 300-second child limit.

Primary descriptive quantity is gross cumulative-counter delta in joules over a fixed measurement window: two seconds pre, complete owned child workflow, two seconds post. Padding also captures settling of the documented one-second averaged power signal on non-GA100 Ampere. Counter samples must be complete and nondecreasing; invalid counter runs remain visible with null primary energy, not a substituted favorable endpoint. Report trapezoidal integration of reported power separately as a diagnostic, not an interchangeable primary endpoint or calibration truth. Missing power produces null integral. Preserve every intended run and failed/partial training result.

The [NVML documentation](https://docs.nvidia.com/deploy/nvml-api/latest/api/group__nvmlDeviceQueries.html) defines the counter in mJ since driver reload and power in mW; reported power is averaged over one second on this architecture family. Sampling faster does not make readings independent or improve hardware accuracy. A constant 100 W over 2 s analytic integration control must yield 200 J. Counter/integral disagreement is reported, not used to select a preferred arm or claim a known sensor error bound.

This is a shared display GPU. Gross readings include background board use, initialization, first-step checks, training/validation and fixed padding; no idle subtraction, exclusive-device, per-process, whole-host, carbon or universal energy-saving claim is justified. No distributional inference or energy test is predeclared from three pairs. Keep this observed scope separate from the original memory/time pilot and any future controlled energy study.
