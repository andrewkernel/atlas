# Equivalence Tests: A Practical Primer for t Tests, Correlations, and Meta-Analyses

- **Authors:** Daniël Lakens.
- **Year / venue:** 2017; Social Psychological and Personality Science 8(4).
- **Verified primary URL:** [Equivalence Tests: A Practical Primer for t Tests, Correlations, and Meta-Analyses](https://doi.org/10.1177/1948550617697177).
- **Read depth:** Publisher abstract and TOST explanation.
- **Objective:** Test absence of practically meaningful effects.
- **Technical method:** Two one-sided tests with predefined equivalence bounds.
- **Main finding used:** Nonsignificance in a difference test is not equivalence.
- **Limitations / Atlas assessment:** Assumptions, margins and adequate power remain necessary.
- **Relevance:** Supports scoped statistical evidence, not scientific certification.
- **Code status:** Accompanying R/spreadsheet tools described; not used.
- **Open question:** Which margins are scientifically meaningful for each claim?
- **Requires further verification:** Small-sample behavior and multiplicity need protocol-specific calibration.
