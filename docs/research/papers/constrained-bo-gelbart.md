# Bayesian Optimization with Unknown Constraints

Michael A. Gelbart, Jasper Snoek and Ryan P. Adams; 2014 preprint. [Versioned abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.5607v1), [versioned HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/1403.5607v1).

Read scope: abstract, motivation, and selected method sections including decoupled observations/cost information (§3.3). No reproduction or full outcome audit. [Captured bytes](../comparison-lineage-sources.json).
