# Time-uniform confidence sequences

Steven R. Howard, Aaditya Ramdas, Jon McAuliffe and Jasjeet Sekhon. **Time-uniform, nonparametric, nonasymptotic confidence sequences.** Annals of Statistics49(2):1055–1080,2021; [arXiv1810.08240](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.08240) first submitted2018, inspected abstract/version metadata(v9,2022). No theorem or implementation audit.

The paper develops confidence sequences with time-uniform coverage under stated probability conditions, connecting sequential concentration and related inference methods.

**Atlas assessment:** adaptively deciding when verification has enough evidence is established statistical territory. Repeated fixed-n intervals are not automatically anytime-valid. Any future cost-aware verification contribution must state its outcome assumptions and use a defensible sequential design; a citation is not a guarantee for arbitrary training losses, dependence or unseen failures. The fixed-count pilot/confirmation design remains the simpler default. No confidence-sequence method was implemented or evaluated here.
