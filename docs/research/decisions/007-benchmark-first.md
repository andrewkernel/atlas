# ADR 007 — Benchmark first; require a distinct policy treatment

Status: accepted research-scope amendment, 2026-10-15. Application and primary-policy implementations remain unbuilt.

## Context

The fair comparator receives the same contracts, adapter facts, native mechanisms and existing checks. Current documents do not specify an additional Atlas next-action function. The [comparison audit](../15-comparison-identifiability.md) explains why contract awareness alone cannot distinguish identical policies. The [BERT QA screen](../workload-screen/qa-resource-pilot.md) strengthens the native baseline while exposing measurement/reference limitations.

## Decision

Prioritize a reproducible evaluation package, workload/contract declarations, independent-label preparation and supported existing-tool integrations. Retain [native finite planning](002-native-planner.md) as an engineering boundary, not evidence of a novel planner. Supersede any reading of H1 as a ready-to-run superiority study: freeze a distinct algorithm, shared information, objective, budget ledger and baseline before confirmation. Do not add a frontend, LLM or generic verifier to supply a missing contribution.

Continue narrowly justified controls resolving objective, reference, measurement or integration uncertainties. A negative comparison or benchmark consolidation/upstream engineering contribution is acceptable. External labels, full useful-experiment access and a primary held-out comparison remain uncompleted gates.

## Consequences

This ADR alone authorizes no new optimization mechanism. Do not weaken the informed baseline or change a strict scientific relation after a favorable resource result. Separate information ablations, ordering algorithms and integration-effort studies. Architecture interfaces remain proposals; bind evidence to the actual observer/executor relation.
